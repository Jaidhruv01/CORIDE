# CoRide Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Problem Statement
Daily commuters and intercity travelers encounter inflated cab tariffs, congested public transit, and fragmented informal carpool coordination on social channels. Drivers travel with empty seats along major corridors incurring unnecessary fuel and toll burdens.

## 2. Platform Value Proposition
CoRide provides a verified, two-sided carpooling marketplace with instant booking, atomic seat inventory protection, transparent cost-sharing fares, in-app coordination chat, 24x7 safety controls, and mutual reputation ratings.

## 3. Roles & Permissions
- **Riders**: Discover routes, filter by time/price/amenities, book seats atomically, pay securely, coordinate via chat, view digital QR ticket passes, submit reviews.
- **Drivers**: Add vehicles, publish upcoming ride itineraries with stopovers, set seat contributions, accept bookings, trigger live trip milestones (*Arrive, Start, Complete*), receive automatic payout allocations.
- **Administrators**: Marketplace metrics oversight, driver document verification queue, incident resolution, financial ledger auditing.
